FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Find answers to common questions about using VQS.

Do I have to keep my browser open while waiting?

Your place in line is securely stored on the server. If you accidentally close your browser, simply sign back in on any device to restore your ticket and position. However, keep the tab open when your turn is near to catch the alert!

Can I enter the queue multiple times?

No. Each verified account may hold only one active queue ticket at a time. This guarantees fairness for all users.

How do I become an administrator?

Administrator privileges are assigned securely by the system owner or via configured administrative keys. Standard user registrations cannot self-assign admin roles.

What should I do if I need to leave before being called?

You can click "Leave Queue" at any time from your client portal ticket. This frees up your spot and moves everyone behind you up automatically.
